What 100% actually means here

Gluten free is how the whole shop is set up, not a separate line of products we keep to one side.

No wheat comes through the door

We don't stock it, so it can't end up on a plate by accident. That includes the bread, the bagels and the crumbs.

One toaster, one grill, one bench

Because there's nothing to separate from, there's no shared equipment risk to manage.

Bagels made fresh each morning

Gluten free bagels that are worth eating on their own terms. They're in the front fridge and they sell out.

Ask us anything

If you're coeliac, tell the person at the bench. They can walk you through exactly how your order is made.

One kitchen, one standard. Everything you can see being made behind the bench is gluten free, from the toast on the grill to the crumbs on the board.

Built around the bench

Foundry sits on Level 3 of Indooroopilly Shopping Centre — timber panelling, live plants above the machine, and a Synesso running from open to close. We pull ristretto shots for milk drinks and make everything to order.

Going fully gluten free wasn't a menu decision, it was a kitchen decision. It means someone who usually has to interrogate a café can just sit down and order.
